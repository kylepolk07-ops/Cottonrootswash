Hi there, and welcome to Cotton Roots and Company. Here is the whole process in just a few simple steps.

Create your customer account and securely verify payment through Stripe.

For your first bag, visit 307 Locust Street with your dirty clothes. Open My Bags, get the bag-box code, take one Cotton Roots bag, and scan its QR code. That assigns the bag to you. Put your clothes inside and create your order.

Choose shop drop-off, or full-service pickup and return. For delivery, save the correct address and instructions. We can serve a home, workplace, camper, or RV within our delivery area. Review the distance fee before confirming.

At drop-off or pickup, the bag is scanned and the handoff is recorded. An approved Washing Specialist accepts the job, scans your bag, photographs the scale, enters the exact weight, and follows your care instructions.

Your items stay together while they are washed, dried, and folded. The same bag is scanned when it is returned to the shop or your delivery address.

You receive a text and app notification when the laundry is ready. Review the final weight and total, then pay securely through Stripe. Scan the matching bag when you collect it, or confirm your delivery in the app.

You also earn Cotton Roots Rewards: one point per paid dollar. After a verified referral's first paid order, both customers receive one hundred points. Every two hundred fifty points earns a five-dollar credit.

That is it: one registered bag, flexible service, clear updates, and fresh laundry. We will be glad to take care of you.
