COTTON ROOTS & CO.
INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT — DRAFT

1. Parties and purpose. This draft describes an independent provider relationship between Cotton Roots & Co. (Company) and the applicant (Contractor). The Company facilitates customer bookings and coordinates laundry services. Final terms are subject to review and execution.

2. Work offered. Depending on the work selected and offered, Contractor may perform wash, dry and fold services, or pickup, wash, fold and return. Contractor may choose whether to accept an offered job. Accepting a job confirms that Contractor can complete all required steps by the deadline shown before acceptance. No separate manager approval is required for each job once onboarding is approved.

3. Service standard. Verify each bag and follow all required QR handoff scans. Keep each customer's items together. Read care labels, follow documented detergent preferences, use clean equipment and folding surfaces, avoid unapproved additives, and return laundry neatly folded in its correctly labeled bag. Do not mix customers' loads. Promptly report damage, missing items, delays, safety issues or an inability to meet the promised deadline.

4. Supplies and machine expenses. Contractor pays washer and dryer charges up front and is responsible for ordinary machine-use expenses under the all-inclusive service rate. Use approved detergents and follow order instructions. The Company will communicate any supplies it provides separately.

5. Compensation and payout. Contractor earns $1.00 per approved completed pound for jobs meeting the applicable service requirements. Approved completed work is calculated from the recorded weight. Payouts run weekly, one full week in arrears: work completed Monday–Sunday is scheduled for Friday of the second following week. Disputes or corrections should be reported promptly to management.

6. Deadlines. Each offer displays a due date and time. Contractor confirms the deadline before accepting. If a delay becomes likely, Contractor must alert management promptly rather than silently missing the due time.

7. Proposed missed-deadline provision — attorney review required. The Company proposes that a missed agreed deadline may result in a $10 service fee and completion of the affected job without compensation, subject to management review, the final signed agreement and applicable law. No fee, forfeiture or deduction is automatically imposed by this preview.

8. Driving and safe work. If performing pickup or return services, Contractor must hold a valid license, maintain legally required vehicle insurance, obey traffic laws, and use safe transport and handling practices. Contractor is responsible for promptly reporting accidents and other incidents. The scope and enforceability of any allocation of responsibility, indemnification or release will be determined in the final attorney-reviewed document; this draft does not purport to waive claims that cannot lawfully be waived.

9. Customer information. Use customer names, addresses, access instructions and contact information only to perform assigned jobs. Do not disclose or reuse personal information. Do not photograph customer belongings except where the approved job process requires documentation.

10. Required onboarding. Before access to jobs, provide requested identity information, signed tax form W-9, any driving documentation required for selected services, and complete the final signed services and safety agreements through secure collection methods.

11. Status and changes. The intended relationship is independent contracting, but legal classification depends on actual working practices and applicable law, not a label alone. Any final changes to these terms must be communicated and accepted through the proper agreement process.

END OF DRAFT. Read and discuss questions with management before signing the attorney-approved final version.
