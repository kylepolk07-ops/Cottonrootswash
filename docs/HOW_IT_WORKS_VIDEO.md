## Voiceover script

Welcome to Cotton Roots & Company Wash & Fold—laundry care built around your schedule. Our Sweetwater location offers walk-in access from 9 AM to 5 PM. For access between 5 PM and 9 PM, call 903-426-1543 before arriving.

Start by creating your Customer account. Choose drop-off service or pickup and return, then add your detergent choice and any special care instructions.

For drop-off, bring your assigned bag to 307 Locust Street. Scan the bag in the app and confirm that you are leaving dirty laundry. The app records each handoff so you can follow your bag from start to finish.

An approved independent Washing Specialist accepts the job, follows your care notes, and updates each step as your laundry is washed, dried, and folded.

When your order is returned, review the final weight, service charges, delivery charge when applicable, and Texas sales tax. Pay securely online.

For shop pickup, scan the bag and confirm that you are collecting clean laundry. For home delivery, the app records delivery and shows a photo when the bag is left at your address.

No waiting at a counter. Clear updates, personal care notes, and online checkout—all in one place. Cotton Roots & Company. One little step. Fresh beginnings.
