# Cotton Roots & Co. — Build Brief for an AI Coding Agent

## Goal
Turn the two attached HTML design prototypes into a secure, production-oriented, mobile-first laundry service. Keep **two separate frontends**: Customer and Contractor. The Contractor frontend contains a separately authenticated **Management** area. One common backend/order state machine connects both. Preserve the cream/sage/black pictogram look and one-question/one-action-per-screen navigation. The provided HTML files are design references, **not** secure or working multi-user backends. Do not copy localStorage as a production datastore or bypass access control with demo login. A later change to either frontend must be tested against shared contract/API and end-to-end flow.

## Delivery approach
Create a source-controlled repository, staging environment and deployment runbook. Prefer a practical managed full-stack setup (e.g. modern web frontend, Supabase or equivalent for Postgres, Auth, storage and server functions, hosted website/PWA). Integrate Square **server-side** with real webhook verification and idempotency, not simulated paid flags. SMS provider for messages and bag codes. Secure storage + limited access for license, insurance, W-9 and signed agreement. Ask owner to connect accounts/credentials securely, never ask to paste secrets into chat/source. No public go-live until privacy, security, legal and actual-device workflow tests pass. Do not represent a prototype as a completed live service.

## Two apps / roles
- Customer: register once; book Drop Off at $2.50/lb or Pick Up/Wash/Fold/Return at $3.00/lb; detergent preference (shop standard, sealed/labeled own detergent, or contact shop); standard/rush request (+$1/lb only after approval); manage bags; guided QR scans; view current order status, final rounded pounds, secure Square checkout; see only own paid-and-ready pickup orders; choose order, scan matching bag, get thank-you / one optional 1–5 star rating per completed order.
- Contractor: invite link and one-time registration (DL, insurance when driving, W-9, services agreement, safety acknowledgment; separate draft pending lawyer). Select Wash & Fold, Pickup+Wash+Return or Both. Initial management onboarding approval required, but no per-job management approval. Two-week tap-to-toggle availability calendar. Eligible offers show an actual deadline and required acknowledgment before acceptance. My Jobs: one step per screen with pictograms; My Pay: pending, approved for payout, paid. $1.00 per approved completed pound, contractor pays washers/dryers up front; payout weekly, one full week in arrears. Separate guided SOP.
- Management: distinct secure role/login, no contractor registration needed; invite/review applicants and documents, approve once, set offers/deadlines, monitor statuses/custody, exceptions, weigh-ins, Square receipt/payment, released bags, payout ledger, ratings. Owner preview/testing account with synthetic data should never bypass live authentication.

## Critical order flow
- First branded bag included. Customer brings dirty clothes in THEIR OWN BAG on first visit and personally transfers clothes into a pre-tagged black Cotton Roots bag from coded box. Request access code for first included bag or buy extra bag $10 (payment before code). Scan QR UNDER box lid = 'I'm taking a bag'; scan unique bag QR (e.g. CRC-0001) to link to own account. Prompt to physically shut and lock box; checkbox acknowledgment only, not proof lock electronically closed. 24h first-bag return clock starts on verified bag CLAIM, not code request. If not returned with first laundry in 24h, proposed $10 bag charge subject to disclosure/authorization. No personal data embedded in public QR.
- Customer shop drop-off: scan unique assigned bag QR, then physically posted shop/wall QR; server validates authenticated bag ownership, eligible order, station and server timestamp. Bag into DIRTY bin. Pickup from customer: assigned contractor scans bag at collection; same contractor handles laundering and return. No guessed drop-off time or guessed weight.
- Contractor SHOP job checkout, on FIRST visit: scan assigned bag -> weigh with easy slider; record measured value and round UP to nearest 0.5 lb (exact half stays exact); confirm recorded weight -> scan shop wall QR to CHECK OUT. No weight photo. Until all required inputs validated, no checkout. Change status to Washing/Pending; customer sees it. Secure server timestamp, event log, custody holder and deadline.
- Contractor can leave the app and return later. SECOND visit: confirm laundering complete -> scan same bag -> scan wall QR CHECK IN at shop. Mark 'Laundry finished / Returned to shop', visible to customer, but do NOT equate it to paid or released.
- Management verifies actual total, initiates Square hosted checkout/invoice, and Square verified webhook marks paid. Only once paid and returned/ready may staff put it in accessible PAID pickup bin; otherwise locked payment-hold area. Customer sees ONLY own paid-and-ready eligible orders; selects one; scans matching bag; incorrect bag blocked; verify identity/ownership, readiness/payment and double-pickup on SERVER; records pickup. Show thank-you and rating.
- Keep contractor payout ledger separate from customer invoice ledger. Job completion earns Pending; when customer payment verified moves to Approved for Payout per business rule; weekly one-week-delayed batch, with exceptions handled by management, no duplicate payouts. Show clear breakdown and dates. Late jobs flagged for owner review; proposed $10 late service fee and unpaid completion are DRAFT legal terms — do not automate withholding/fees without attorney review and lawful setup.

## Tests to implement before launch
1. First bag claim and lock acknowledgment; repeated QR/duplicate bag forbidden; 24h clock from claim.
2. Dropoff bag+wall scans; wrong account or wall code rejected.
3. Contractor initial approval once, eligible offer/deadline acknowledgment, availability matches offers.
4. Job checkout requires bag, rounded weight, wall in exact sequence; washing status appears customer side.
5. Returning later to same job requires matching bag and wall check-in; status finished not yet paid.
6. Square sandbox payment event/webhook -> paid/ready release; wrong bag/unpaid/double pickup blocked; thank you/rating exactly once.
7. Earnings pending -> approved after verified payment -> weekly arrears ledger -> paid; no double counting.
8. Owner sees exceptions and feedback, not exposed to public; customers cannot view other customers or DL/W-9.
9. Both frontends tested on actual iOS Safari and Android Chrome, plus camera permissions and fallback.

## Working method and handoff
Build incrementally in staging: shared schema/auth and role test accounts; bag/orders/custody; contractor workflow; customer flow; payments/payouts; onboarding/docs; notifications; testing; launch. Keep staging and production separate. Commit changes, tests and migration scripts to repo. Provide owner: staging URLs, test credentials through secure channel, deployment instructions, environment variables inventory (names only), owner-facing changelog and a screen-by-screen test checklist. Do not make live financial, legal or customer-facing changes without explicit owner approval. Ask owner for service-account logins only when needed. If unavailable, build/test in sandbox and clearly report what is blocked. Do not claim overnight launch unless all dependencies, approvals and tests are actually complete.
